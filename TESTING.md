# Ridepane car test

Open [ridepane.beantown-usa.com](https://ridepane.beantown-usa.com/) on the Tesla screen. Run the checks on that screen; a phone browser measures the phone, not the car. The driver must keep attention on the road. A passenger can handle the tests while driving; do the initial setup while parked.

1. **Pair and browse:** If asked to sign in, scan Ridepane's QR code with your phone and approve the car. Open a Plex library and a title. Open **Full YouTube** and confirm that your signed-in home page, playlists, subscriptions, Likes, and Watch Later appear. Record any Google sign-in problem.
2. **Check browser and audio:** Open **Car checks** and tap **Check browser**. Photograph the report. It records video APIs, audio codec hints, reported audio channel count, storage quota, and fullscreen API. Then choose **Stereo**, **5.1**, or **7.1** and tap **Play channel test** while parked. The quiet tones play one channel at a time. Note whether they move between distinct speaker positions, repeat in the same speakers, or are silent. Channel 4 in 5.1/7.1 uses a low tone for the bass channel. A capability report alone does not prove surround output, and Ridepane's current video audio remains stereo.
3. **Check cellular:** Disconnect the car from Wi-Fi, confirm cellular service, and tap **Check connection** two or three times. Photograph the results. This transfers 8 MiB per run through your Nginx proxy to Unraid. If you also use a phone hotspot, repeat once on the hotspot and label the photo. The number is a snapshot; it will change along the route.
4. **Check cache:** While on the car screen, tap **Save 64 MB test cache**. Wait for completion and photograph the result. Leave the browser, reopen Ridepane, return to **Car checks**, and tap **Verify saved cache**. Photograph that result. If possible, verify again after the car has been parked for a while. Tap **Clear test cache** when finished. This tests browser storage only; Plex offline playback is not implemented.
5. **Check Plex playback:** Play a familiar title at **High** for at least five minutes on cellular. Note startup time, pauses, picture quality, audio, and lip sync. Repeat at **Maximum** in a well-covered area. The current targets are 5 Mbps and 10 Mbps video, with peaks of 6.5 Mbps and 13 Mbps, plus 320 kbps audio. Try a poor-signal stretch if convenient.
6. **Check Full YouTube:** From Ridepane, open **Full YouTube**, select a video from a playlist on the car screen, start audio if prompted, and try the available fullscreen control. If the remote screen stays black, return to Ridepane and tap **YouTube fallback** to try JPEG frames. Note whether the Selkies controls appear, whether the Tesla browser chrome remains, whether sound works, and whether playback pauses or becomes blurry. Use **Full YouTube** again to restore H.264 in that browser.

The first useful report is a photo of **Car checks** on cellular, a photo of the cache verification result, and a short note saying which Plex quality and Full YouTube tests worked. Do not share your Ridepane password, Plex token, or Google account details.
