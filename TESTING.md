# Ridepane car test

Open [ridepane.beantown-usa.com](https://ridepane.beantown-usa.com/) on the Tesla screen. Run the checks on that screen; a phone browser measures the phone, not the car. The driver must keep attention on the road. A passenger can handle the tests while driving; do the initial setup while parked.

1. **Pair and browse:** If asked to sign in, scan Ridepane's QR code with your phone and approve the car. On the phone pairing page, use **Open Full Plex on this phone to sign in** once, then do the same for YouTube if desired. On the Tesla, tap **Open Full Plex** and confirm Plex Home, Continue Watching, search, your libraries, and the player appear. Open **Full YouTube** and check your signed-in home page and playlists. The two sites share one remote browser, so opening one replaces the other on its screen.
2. **Check browser and audio:** Open **Car checks** and tap **Check browser**. Photograph the report. It records video APIs, audio codec hints, reported audio channel count, storage quota, and fullscreen API. Then choose **Stereo**, **5.1**, or **7.1** and tap **Play channel test** while parked. The quiet tones play one channel at a time. Note whether they move between distinct speaker positions, repeat in the same speakers, or are silent. Channel 4 in 5.1/7.1 uses a low tone for the bass channel. A capability report alone does not prove surround output, and Ridepane's current video audio remains stereo.
3. **Check cellular:** Disconnect the car from Wi-Fi, confirm cellular service, and tap **Check connection** two or three times. Photograph the results. This transfers 8 MiB per run through your Nginx proxy to Unraid. If you also use a phone hotspot, repeat once on the hotspot and label the photo. The number is a snapshot; it will change along the route.
4. **Check cache:** While on the car screen, tap **Save 64 MB test cache**. Wait for completion and photograph the result. Leave the browser, reopen Ridepane, return to **Car checks**, and tap **Verify saved cache**. Photograph that result. If possible, verify again after the car has been parked for a while. Tap **Clear test cache** when finished. This tests browser storage only; Plex offline playback is not implemented.
5. **Check Full Plex playback:** Full Plex should open in the JPEG mode that displayed on the Tesla. Swipe up and down with one finger; the Plex page should scroll instead of selecting text. Tap a title, try **Keyboard** for search, then **Hide keyboard**. Play a familiar title for at least five minutes on cellular. If it pauses, return to Ridepane and try **Cellular mode** for the same title; this targets 720p, 24 fps, and 4 Mbps. Compare smoothness, clarity, and lip sync. The regular **Open Full Plex** button restores your previous quality settings for Wi-Fi. Note startup time, pauses, picture quality, Plex controls, subtitles, and whether Continue Watching updates. Try the fullscreen control. If the remote screen remains black, use **Plex quick play** and report which path worked.
6. **Check Plex quick play:** Open **Plex quick play**, select TV, then tap **Back**; it should return to the library list. Play a title at **High** and check lip sync. If audio is ahead, move **Delay sound** right in 50 ms steps until speech looks aligned; note the value and whether it remains aligned. The alternative **384 kbps MP2 · sync test** mode is also available. Repeat at **Maximum** only in a well-covered area. The video targets are 5 Mbps and 10 Mbps, with peaks of 6.5 Mbps and 13 Mbps.
7. **Check Full YouTube:** From Ridepane, open **Full YouTube** in its default JPEG mode. Swipe through the signed-in home page and playlists with one finger, select a video, start audio if prompted, and try the available fullscreen control. Test **Keyboard** and **Hide keyboard** in search. Note whether the controls appear, whether Tesla browser chrome remains, whether sound works, and whether playback pauses or becomes blurry. The optional **Try H.264** button is only for comparison; the normal button restores JPEG.

The first useful report is a photo of **Car checks** on cellular, a photo of the cache verification result, and a short note saying whether Full Plex and Full YouTube displayed and played. Do not share your Ridepane password, Plex token, or account details.
